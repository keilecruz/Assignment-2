Student Life

Fall Student Organization Fair

September 24, 2026 | 11:00 AM - 2:00 PM

The Fall Student Organization Fair is a great opportunity for upcoming students to discover campus clubs, meet other students, and become involved in the university community!

About the Event

Student Organization from Birchmere University will gather together at the University Quad, where they will introduce their club and activities to students. Their are so many different types of clubs that vary from academics, culture, recreation, volunteering, or social activities that will be introduced.

Students can visit organization tables, ask questions, meet current members, and learn how to become involved throughout the semester.

What to Expect

Students attending the fair can expect:

  • Information tables from different student organizations.
  • Opportunities to meet club members.
  • Information about upcoming club meetings.
  • Activities and giveaways at selected tables.
  • Information about how to join organizations.

Schedule

  1. 11:00 AM — Event begins and organization tables open.
  2. 12:00 PM — Student organization introductions and activities.
  3. 1:00 PM — Club information session.
  4. 2:00 PM — Event concludes.

Accessibility & Participation

The University Quad is accessible to students using mobility devices. Accessible seating and clear paths will be available throughout the event area.

Students who are in need of additional accommodations can contact the Office of Student Engagement before attending the event.
